Evidence 09 - Gas scaling. Recorded 2026-10-21 on main after 49a0d59; contracts/DutchAuction.sol last changed in 2c450c4.
Purpose: back the claim that no call costs more as the number of bidders grows (no loops over bidders),
the gas-limit / denial-of-service concern from Module 3 Lecture 2 (slides 13-15).

five calls at the same point in the auction's life. Tolerance: spread <= 1% of the cheapest run.
